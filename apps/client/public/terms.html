Gur is a free tile rummy game, played the way it is in Kosovo. It is made and run by Ubejd Morina ("I", "me"). By playing Gur you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the game.

1. A game, and only a game

Gur is played for fun. There is no real money in it: nothing to bet, nothing to buy and nothing to win except the game. Points have no value outside the game.

2. Accounts

  • You can play without an account. Signing in with Google is your choice.
  • An account is for one person. Do not share it, and do not sign in as somebody else.
  • Your username is public. Do not pick one that insults people, pretends to be somebody else, or is hateful or obscene. I may change or remove a username that does.

What Gur keeps about you is described in the privacy policy.

3. Fair play

  • Do not cheat, and do not use programs that play for you or show you what you should not see.
  • Do not use a changed copy of the game to play with others.
  • Do not team up with another player against the others at a table, for example by telling each other your tiles. This matters most online, where the others may be strangers.
  • Do not attack, overload or disturb the game's servers.
  • Treat the people you play with decently.

4. Messages and the chat at a table

Friends can write to each other, and the players at a table can talk to each other. In both:

  • no bullying, harassment, threats or hate against anyone;
  • no sexual content, and nothing that puts children at risk;
  • no spam, scams, or links to harmful sites;
  • no sharing of other people's private information.

If somebody bothers you, block them: you stop being friends, and neither of you can write to the other any more. Report anything that breaks these rules from the conversation (Report). At a table, you can mute any player for yourself, and, when both of you signed in, report them from the chat. Reports are looked at, and accounts that break the rules are removed.

I may limit or remove the account of anyone who breaks these terms, remove them from games, and delete messages that break them.
