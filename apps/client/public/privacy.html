Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Gur is a free tile rummy game, played the way it is in Kosovo. It is made and run by Ubejd Morina ("I", "me"). This page says what the game knows about you and what happens with it.

In short

  • You can play without an account. Then no personal information about you is kept.
  • If you sign in with Google, Gur keeps your e-mail address, your name and your picture, plus the username and player ID it gives you.
  • There are no ads and no tracking. Nothing about you is sold or given to advertisers.

1. What Gur keeps

If you play as a guest

  • The name you type when you play with friends. The other players in your lobby see it. Your browser remembers it so you do not have to type it again.

If you sign in with Google

  • From Google: your e-mail address, your name, your profile picture and your Google account's ID number.
  • Made by Gur: a username (it starts as your Google name; you can change it) and an eight-digit player ID.
  • When your account was made and when you last signed in.
  • Your friends: who you asked to be friends, who asked you, and who accepted. The other player sees your request and, once you are friends, has you on their list.

Gur never sees your Google password, your contacts or anything else in your Google account.

Games

  • A game with friends runs on the game server: who sits in the lobby, the tiles and the moves. This is held only while the game lasts. It is not saved, and it is gone when the lobby closes.
  • A game against bots runs on your own device and is sent nowhere.
  • While you are signed in and have the game open, the game server knows that, so your friends can invite you into their lobby. It is not saved anywhere, and it is forgotten when you close the game or sign out.

Technical records

  • Like every website, the companies that host Gur record technical details of each visit, such as the IP address, the kind of browser and the time. I do not use these to find out who you are.

On your device

  • Your browser keeps what is needed for the game to work: that you are signed in, the name you typed, and the key that lets you back into a game after a lost connection. Gur uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

2. What other players can see

Your username, your player ID and your picture are public: the people you play with see them, and anyone can look a player up by them. Your e-mail address is never shown to other players.

3. What it is used for

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To show the other players who is at the table.
  • To keep usernames unique, and to deal with cheating or abuse.

The information Gur receives from Google is used only to make and show your profile. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is shared with nobody except the service providers below, who only store or carry it so that the game can work.

4. Who stores it

Gur runs on services from these companies. Each handles the information under its own privacy policy.

  • Supabase: keeps the accounts (sign-in and profiles).
  • Google: signs you in, if you choose to.
  • Render: runs the game server.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.

Their computers may be in a country other than yours.

5. How long it is kept

  • Your account: until you delete it.
  • Games: only while they are being played.
  • Technical records: for as long as the hosting companies keep them.

6. Your choices

  • You can play without signing in.
  • You can change your username and sign out on the first screen of the game.
  • You can delete your account yourself, at any time: on the first screen of the game, choose Delete next to your name. Your sign-in, your username, your player ID and your picture are removed at once and for good.
  • If you cannot get into the game, write to me from the e-mail address of the account and I delete it for you within 30 days.
  • You can take away Gur's access to your Google account at any time in your Google account's settings.
  • You can ask me what is kept about you, or to have it corrected.

7. Children

Gur is not aimed at young children. To sign in you must be old enough to have your own Google account in your country.

8. Changes to this page

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If the change is an important one, I will point it out in the game.
